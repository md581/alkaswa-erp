# AL KASWA HOLDINGS ERP — User Manual & Operational Guide

## Overview
Welcome to the official User Manual for **AL KASWA HOLDINGS ERP**. This guide provides step-by-step instructions for departmental personnel, managers, site engineers, sales executives, legal team members, and accounting staff to perform daily operations efficiently and in compliance with corporate governance standards.

---

## 1. System Navigation & Access

### Logging In
1. Open the ERP web application URL in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Safari.
2. Select your authorized **Role** from the user identity dropdown or log in with your credentials.
3. Upon authentication, you will be navigated to the module workspace designated for your role.

### Enterprise Switcher & Language Support
* **Bilingual Toggle**: Click the **EN / বাংলা** toggle in the top navigation bar at any time to switch the interface text between English and Bengali.
* **Notifications Bell**: Click the bell icon in the top header to view real-time system notifications regarding due installments, pending workflow approvals, document expiry alerts, and site updates.

## 2. Module Operational Guides

### Module 1: Sales CRM & Customer Booking
* **Adding a New Lead**:
  1. Navigate to **Sales & CRM** -> **Leads**.
  2. Click **Add New Lead**. Enter Customer Name, Mobile Number, Email, Lead Source (Facebook, Referral, Walk-In, Broker), Interested Project, and Budget.
  3. Click **Save Lead**.
* **Scheduling a Site Visit**:
  1. Open the lead profile, select **Schedule Site Visit**.
  2. Choose the date, time, and assigned sales executive. Upon completion, mark the visit feedback as 'Completed'.
* **Executing a Property Booking**:
  1. Navigate to **Bookings** -> **Create Booking**.
  2. Select the Customer, Project, and available Unit (Flat/Plot).
  3. Enter Total Agreed Price, Booking Money Amount, Downpayment, and Installment Frequency (Monthly / Quarterly).
  4. Click **Generate Booking**. This automatically creates the installment payment schedule.
* **Issuing a Money Receipt**:
  1. Navigate to **Finance** -> **Money Receipts**.
  2. Click **New Receipt**. Select the Booking ID and Payer Name.
  3. Enter Received Amount, Payment Mode (Cash, Cheque, Pay Order, Bank Transfer), Bank Name, and Instrument Number.
  4. Click **Issue Receipt**. Print or download the official PDF receipt for the customer.

---

### Module 2: Customer Self-Service Portal
* **Viewing Account Statement**:
  1. Customers log in using their registered Phone Number and Booking ID.
  2. The dashboard displays Total Property Value, Total Paid, Outstanding Balance, and Next Due Installment.
* **Downloading Documents**:
  1. Go to **My Documents** tab to view and download digitally signed Money Receipts, Booking Agreements, Allocation Letters, and NOCs.
* **Submitting a Customer Support Ticket**:
  1. Go to **Support Tickets** -> **Create Ticket**.
  2. Select Category (Billing, Construction Query, Handover, Legal/Mutation). Enter Subject and Details.
  3. Track live staff response and status updates ('Open', 'In Progress', 'Resolved').

---

### Module 3: Land Sourcing & Legal CS/SA/RS/BS Vault
* **Recording Land Acquisition**:
  1. Navigate to **Land Sourcing** -> **Land Vault**.
  2. Click **Add Land Record**. Fill in Location, Size (in Decimal/Katha), Mouza, JL Number, CS Dag, SA Dag, RS Dag, and BS/City Dag numbers.
  3. Upload scanned land deeds, Porcha, and Mutation documents.
* **Tracking Mutation & JVA Execution**:
  1. Under **Mutation Management**, enter the Mutation Case Number, Land Office details, and Submission Date.
  2. Update status from 'Submitted' -> 'Under Review' -> 'Approved'.
  3. For Joint Venture Agreements (JVA), record Developer Share %, Landowner Share %, and Power of Attorney registration details.

---

### Module 4: Project Management & BOQ Construction Tracker
* **Managing Construction BOQ**:
  1. Navigate to **Construction & Projects** -> **Bill of Quantities (BOQ)**.
  2. Enter required materials (Cement, Rod/Steel, Bricks, Sand, Tiles, Electrical/Plumbing).
  3. Set Estimated Quantities and Unit Rates.
* **Submitting Daily Site Logs**:
  1. Site Engineers open **Daily Site Log**.
  2. Select Project, Date, Weather conditions, Active Laborer Count, Materials Consumed today, and Work Completed notes.
  3. Attach site photos. Click **Submit Log**.

---

### Module 5: Procurement & Store Inventory Management
* **Creating a Purchase Requisition**:
  1. Go to **Procurement** -> **Requisitions** -> **New Requisition**.
  2. Select Project, Required Material, Quantity, and Required-By Date.
  3. Submit for GM / MD approval.
* **Store Inventory & Stock Transfers**:
  1. Storekeepers log material receipts under **Store Management** -> **Receive Stock**.
  2. To transfer stock between Central Store and Project Store, click **Stock Transfer**, select source and destination, specify material and quantity, and submit for verification.

---

### Module 6: Investor Portal & Dividend Management
* **Tracking Capital & Portfolio**:
  1. Investors view total capital invested, current portfolio valuation, and cumulative dividend ROI.
* **Processing Dividend Disbursal**:
  1. Accounts Officers select **Dividend Declarations** -> **New Declaration**.
  2. Enter Total Pool Amount in BDT and target period (e.g., Q1 2026).
  3. Click **Approve & Calculate Payouts**. System auto-calculates individual investor shares.

---

### Module 7: HR, Payroll & Attendance
* **Syncing Biometric Attendance**:
  1. Go to **HR Management** -> **Attendance Sync**.
  2. System auto-ingests check-in/out logs from connected ZKTeco or Hikvision devices.
* **Generating Monthly Payroll**:
  1. Go to **Payroll** -> **Generate Monthly Salary**.
  2. Select Month and Year. System calculates Basic Salary + Allowances + Earned Commissions - Deductions.
  3. Submit for HR Manager and CFO approval before releasing bank transfers.
